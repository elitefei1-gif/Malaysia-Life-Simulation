How to play

  • Each turn is one year. Spend your action points on activities, then press Next year.
  • Your parents' genes, family income and choices shape exam results, careers, love and money.
  • Knowledge starts fresh at every school stage; your Smarts and Discipline make each hour of study count for more.
  • Real Malaysian milestones: SPM, Matrikulasi/STPM/A-Levels, PTPTN, EPF, festivals and open houses.
  • History happens around you: the 1997 crisis, COVID-19 lockdowns, floods, booms and crashes.
  • When you die, continue as your child in Dynasty mode and inherit your estate.
